# TargetBridge 3.6.2

Stable maintenance patch. Build 20261115000349.

**Packages are ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.**
macOS may show security warnings or require privacy permissions again after an
update. Permission continuity is not guaranteed. Do not overwrite a persistently
signed installation with these packages.

## Correction

- Restore the Receiver's 4 MiB TCP receive-window budget used in 3.4.2, instead
  of the 8/6 MiB requests introduced before 3.6.1. The parser still assembles
  large packets across reads; encoded packets are not intentionally discarded.
- This exposes slow-reader backpressure earlier, limiting stale-video buildup
  before the newest-decoded-frame presentation policy can act.
- Preserve nonblocking sockets, TCP_NODELAY, keepalive, decoder behavior,
  packet ordering and the existing Sender presets/heartbeat implementation.
- Add a native receive-window regression test and repeatable isolated transport
  and frame-pacing probes. Sender and Receiver version stamps agree on 3.6.2.

## Evidence And Limits

In a controlled localhost workload with the production socket-accept code,
frame-sized records, periodic large records and a simulated slow reader, the
restored budget reduces p95 record age by roughly 23-26% on the two test Macs.
Throughput remains similar and every tested byte/record stays in order.
These are transport measurements, not measured mouse-to-display latency.

**Issue #237 remains open.** This patch corrects a reproduced queue-budget
regression; it does not establish the complete cause of the reporter's 5K48
Receiver Master lag or certify that their custom Ventura build is fixed.
Mixed-version tests and real input/display latency measurements remain needed.
The frame-rate limiter and capture queue depth are not changed speculatively.
